Course · Borgo.tv Training
Nuke Intermediate — NUK201
Magno Borgo · Borgo.tv Training · 40 hours over 8 weeks

Hands-on compositing training in Nuke 17 with weekly shot-based projects, eight live sessions with Magno Borgo for Q&A and shot discussion, and individual video feedback to help refine each project.

  • SplineWarp and morphing — Image morphs, spline-based matchmoving, and shot stabilization.
  • Digital makeup and image quality — RotoPaint cleanup, skin smoothing and texture preservation, transform concatenation, and filtering.
  • Lens distortion and 2D stabilization — Complex lens distortion, STMap-based undistortion and redistortion, and camera smoothing with 2D tracking.
  • 3D camera stabilization — 3D camera tracking to stabilize shots and smooth camera motion.
  • Tracking refinement and screen replacement — Refining tracks and integrating phone and monitor inserts into defocused footage with limited tracking detail, matching defocus and color to the plate.
  • Grain reconstruction and ModelBuilder — Rebuilding grain with DasGrain, creating and editing geometry from tracked footage, and using UVs, textures, and 3D projections for cleanup and matte painting.
  • Color matching and motion blur — Color matching and look development with MatchGrade, plus 2D and 3D motion blur with VectorBlur, VectorGenerator, and MotionBlur3D.
  • Expressions and Tcl automation — Trigonometric expressions, loops, conditions, animation curve editing, automated slates and file paths, and explorations of Voronoi patterns and STMaps.
